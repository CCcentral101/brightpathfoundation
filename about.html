About Us

       

Bright Path Cancer Foundation is a 501(c)(3)-pending Delaware non-stock nonprofit corporation (incorporated October 16, 2025, File #10369174), focused exclusively on relieving distress for children (0–18) diagnosed with cancer and children in families affected by a parent or sibling’s cancer diagnosis.

       

Our commitment: 100% volunteer-run at launch, no salaries, full transparency with every dollar tracked and annual impact reports.
